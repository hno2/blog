## Workshop examples

I design and facilitate workshops around each group's needs and goals. Topics below are examples, not a fixed programme. I also run workshops with Samuel Wagner through [Doppeldenken](https://doppeldenken.de).

### Storytelling

Learn how to shape ideas into clear, engaging stories. We work on narrative structure and ways to connect with an audience, whether you're communicating in business, education, or everyday life.

### Presentation skills

Prepare and deliver presentations with a clear purpose. We work on structuring content, identifying key messages, and adapting them to an audience. Sessions include practice and feedback.

### Stage confidence

Build confidence through practical exercises in speaking, presence, and improvisation. Practice responding to unexpected moments in a supportive setting.

### Science communication

Make complex topics easier to understand and engaging to hear. Explore plain language, storytelling, and formats such as the Science Slam.

### Improvisation at work

Use improvisation exercises to practise listening, responding, and adapting. Sessions can focus on communication, collaboration, or creative problem-solving.

## Personal coaching

One-to-one coaching for presentations, pitches, and workshop design. We focus on your goals and work through the material together, with practical feedback and time to practise.

Topics can include:

* Presentation structure and delivery
* Pitching an idea
* Designing and facilitating interactive workshops
* Building confidence in communication
* Storytelling
